About My Game

The PHP Vault is an escape-room game where players have to solve three different puzzles to get out. Each room has its own puzzle, and players have to get the answer right before moving on.

What I Worked On

Since I'm working alone with my professor's approval, I was responsible for putting the game together. I worked on the PHP code, created the different pages, and used HTML and CSS to design the website. I also tested the game to make sure everything worked and uploaded it to CODD and GitHub.
